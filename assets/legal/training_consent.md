# Allele Training Data Consent

Version 0.1 · 26 November 2026

## TL;DR

- You are giving **Alexander Leslie, doing business as Allele Jeans Company ("Allele")** photos, a short turning video, your height and your body measurements, so that Allele can train and test its body-measuring app.
- These are **sensitive personal information** under California law (body images and measurements). Allele uses them **only** to build, test and improve its sizing and fit software and products.
- Allele will **not sell or share** your images with anyone outside Allele unless you also sign the separate **Dataset Licence and Trust Agreement**. Even then, faces, tattoos, birthmarks and other identifying features are blurred or removed first.
- People and tools working **for** Allele (staff, contractors, cloud storage, and software tools including AI assistants) may handle your data, but only for Allele's purposes and under confidentiality.
- **You can withdraw at any time** by emailing Administrator@allelejeans.com. Allele deletes your data within 45 days. Models already trained with it cannot be "untrained".
- **Your reward:** a **10% discount on one future Allele order**, if Allele brings products to market. You get it whether or not you join the Dataset Trust, and you keep it if you later withdraw.
- You must be **18 or older**. Taking part is voluntary. California law governs this agreement.

## 1. Who we are

"Allele" means Alexander Leslie, doing business as Allele Jeans Company, Fair Oaks, California. Contact: Administrator@allelejeans.com.

## 2. What we collect

- Photos (front and side) and a turning video of you in fitted clothing or underwear.
- Your height, optional weight and sex, and tape-measure readings, including where each was taken.
- Your contact details and your electronic signature.
- Device information (phone model, camera settings, phone tilt during capture).

## 3. How we use it

Only to develop, train, test, calibrate and improve Allele's body-measurement, sizing and fit software and products. This includes making body models from your images and comparing them with your tape measurements. We do not use your data for advertising, for decisions about credit, employment, insurance or housing, or to identify you.

## 4. Who can see it

- Allele staff and contractors who need it for the purposes above, under confidentiality obligations.
- Service providers that store or process data for Allele, including software and AI tools, under contracts that limit them to Allele's instructions.
- No one else, unless you sign the Dataset Licence and Trust Agreement, or the law requires it (for example, a court order).

Allele does not sell or share your personal information, as those terms are defined by California law, except as described in the Dataset Licence and Trust Agreement if you sign it.

## 5. Anonymisation

Before any image leaves Allele (only possible under the Dataset Agreement), Allele will cover, blur or remove faces, identifying tattoos, birthmarks and other obvious identifying features, and will remove your name and contact details.

## 6. Security and retention

Allele protects your data with reasonable security measures. If there is a breach involving your data, Allele will tell you as required by law.

Allele keeps your data only while it is reasonably needed for the purposes in section 3, and reviews that need at least every 3 years. It deletes the data when it is no longer needed or when you withdraw, whichever comes first.

## 7. Your rights

Under California law (CCPA/CPRA) you can:
- ask what Allele holds about you;
- ask Allele to correct it or delete it;
- limit the use of your sensitive personal information;
- withdraw this consent.

Allele will not treat you differently for using these rights. To use them, email Administrator@allelejeans.com. Allele will respond within 45 days.

**Withdrawal:** Allele deletes your images, video and measurements and stops using them. Withdrawal cannot undo:
- software or models already trained using them (they do not contain your images);
- anonymised copies already licensed under the Dataset Agreement before you withdrew.

## 8. Reward

If Allele brings products to market, you will receive a 10% discount on one future order. It is sent to the email address you provide, and you keep it even if you withdraw. This is in addition to any payments under the Dataset Licence and Trust Agreement, if you sign it. No other payment is owed under this agreement.

## 9. Your promises

- You are 18 or older.
- You are the person in the images.
- You are taking part voluntarily.
- You will not include images of anyone else.

## 10. Electronic signature

You agree to sign electronically. Your electronic signature has the same effect as a handwritten one (U.S. E-SIGN Act and California Uniform Electronic Transactions Act). You can ask for a paper copy at Administrator@allelejeans.com at no cost.

## 11. General

- **Governing law:** California law governs this agreement.
- **Severability:** if any part of it is found invalid or unenforceable, that part is changed only as much as needed to make it enforceable, and the rest stays in effect.
- **Entire agreement:** this is the entire agreement about Allele's internal use of your data. The Dataset Licence and Trust Agreement, if you sign it, separately covers dataset licensing.
- **Changes:** Allele will ask for your consent again before using your data in a materially different way.
- **Other states:** if you live outside California and your state gives you more protection, Allele will follow that law.
