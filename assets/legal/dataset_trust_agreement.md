# Allele Body Dataset Licence and Trust Agreement

Version 0.1 · 26 November 2026 · Optional: separate from the Training Data Consent

## TL;DR

- This is **optional**. Without it, your data stays inside Allele (see the Training Data Consent).
- If you sign, your **anonymised** images and measurements become part of the **Allele Body Dataset**. The dataset may be licensed or sold to others, and the money goes into the **Allele Body Dataset Trust** (the "Trust"), which is separate from Allele.
- **Every contributor who signs gets one equal share** of all dataset revenue (royalties, licence fees, sale proceeds and similar). Allele manages the Trust as a **fiduciary**: in contributors' interest, not its own.
- Allele will only license, sell or distribute the dataset if it judges **in good faith** that doing so is to contributors' advantage.
- **Faces, identifying tattoos, birthmarks and other obvious identifying features are blurred or removed**, along with names and contact details, before anything is shared outside Allele.
- You may name a **beneficiary** to receive your share. **Keep your contact details up to date.** Allele makes good-faith efforts to pay each share within one year. Where the law allows, a share that cannot be delivered within that year is re-pooled for the other contributors.
- There is **no guarantee of any money**. You pay nothing, and this is not an investment. You can leave the dataset for future deals at any time.

## 1. Parties and the Trust

This agreement is between you (the "Contributor") and Alexander Leslie, doing business as Allele Jeans Company ("Allele"), acting as trustee of the Allele Body Dataset Trust.

Before any licence, sale or distribution, Allele will set up the Trust under a written trust instrument consistent with this agreement. The Trust's money and the dataset will be held separately from Allele's own assets and accounts. Allele owes the Trust's beneficiaries the duties of a trustee under California law, including loyalty, prudence, impartiality and keeping records.

## 2. What you contribute

You grant the Trust a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use, copy, anonymise, license, sell and distribute your anonymised images, video and measurements (your "Contribution") as part of the dataset. You keep ownership of your likeness and your personal data.

## 3. Anonymisation and privacy

Before any part of the dataset is shared with anyone outside Allele:
- faces, identifying tattoos, birthmarks and other obvious identifying features are covered, blurred or otherwise anonymised;
- names, contact details and signatures are removed.

Your contact details are used only to run the Trust and pay you. They are never part of the dataset.

Licensees must agree in writing:
- not to try to re-identify anyone;
- to keep the data secure;
- to use it only as licensed.

## 4. When the dataset may be licensed

Allele will offer, license, sell or otherwise distribute the dataset, for money or otherwise, only if it judges in good faith that the deal is to the advantage of the Trust's beneficiaries. In making that judgement it considers price, the licensee's purpose and reputation, privacy protections, and risks to contributors.

Allele's own internal use under the Training Data Consent is not a licence and produces no Trust revenue.

## 5. Your share

- Each Contributor who signs this agreement holds **one equal share**, however many scans they made.
- All revenue the Trust receives from licensing, selling or otherwise distributing the dataset ("Dataset Revenue") is divided equally among the shares entitled to it.
- Revenue from a particular deal is shared among the Contributors whose Contribution was included in that deal.
- If a deal does not identify individual Contributions, the revenue is shared among all shares current at the time of the deal.
- [OPTIONAL — DECIDE BEFORE USE: "Reasonable, documented costs of the deal and of administering the Trust (for example legal, accounting and payment fees) may be deducted before division." Delete this paragraph if no costs may be deducted.]

## 6. Payment

- Allele will make good-faith efforts to pay each share within one year of the Trust receiving the revenue. Payment uses the contact and payment details you provide.
- You must keep your contact details current, and you may need to provide tax information (for example IRS Form W-9). Payments may be reported to tax authorities as required by law.
- If a share cannot be paid within that year because Allele cannot reach you or your beneficiary after good-faith efforts:
  - the share is forfeited and re-pooled for distribution to the remaining beneficiaries, as far as the law permits;
  - where the law instead requires unclaimed funds to be reported or handed to a government (for example under California's Unclaimed Property Law), Allele will do that.
- Allele will give any beneficiary an annual statement of the Trust's revenue and distributions on request.

## 7. Beneficiary

You may name a beneficiary to receive your share after your death, and you may change that person by writing to Administrator@allelejeans.com. If you name no one, your share goes to your estate.

## 8. Leaving the dataset

You may withdraw your Contribution from future deals at any time by emailing Administrator@allelejeans.com:
- Allele removes it from the dataset for any later deal.
- Copies already licensed before you withdrew cannot be recalled.
- You keep your share of revenue from deals that included your Contribution.

Withdrawing from the Training Data Consent also withdraws you from this agreement.

## 9. No guarantee

There is no promise that the dataset will ever be licensed or earn money. This agreement:
- is not employment;
- is not an investment, and you pay nothing for your share;
- does not create a partnership with Allele.

Your 10% product discount under the Training Data Consent is separate, and is not affected by this agreement.

## 10. If the Trust ends

If the Trust ends, Allele distributes any remaining Trust money equally among the beneficiaries entitled to it, and deletes or returns the dataset as the trust instrument requires.

## 11. Electronic signature

You agree to sign electronically. Your electronic signature has the same effect as a handwritten one (U.S. E-SIGN Act and California Uniform Electronic Transactions Act). You can ask for a paper copy at Administrator@allelejeans.com at no cost.

## 12. General

- **Governing law:** California law governs this agreement and the Trust.
- **Severability:** if any part of this agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, that part is changed only as much as needed to make it enforceable, and the rest stays in effect.
- **Changes:** changes that reduce Contributors' rights need each affected Contributor's written consent.
- **Entire agreement:** this agreement, the trust instrument and the Training Data Consent are the entire agreement on these subjects.
- **Successors:** if Allele is acquired or stops trading, its duties as trustee pass to a successor trustee bound by this agreement.
